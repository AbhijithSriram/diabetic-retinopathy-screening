About DrishtiTrust

A health worker photographs each eye at a primary health centre. The server checks that the photo is clear, then grades diabetic retinopathy on the ICDR scale and marks lesions it can see.

Each eye gets one of four outcomes: re-screen in {{ cfg.recall_months }} months, a remote check by an eye specialist, referral to an eye hospital, or an in-person check when no clear photo could be taken. A random 2% of re-screen results are also checked by a specialist.

Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement 26038, sponsored by MathWorks.
