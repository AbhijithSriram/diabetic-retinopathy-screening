Clinical Protocols & Standards

Frequently Answered Questions

Clinical guidelines, tele-ophthalmology safety rules, and patient privacy standards.

  • What is the clinical criteria for an immediate tele-review escalation?
    Any image triggering a Severe NPDR (Grade 3), Proliferative DR (Grade 4), or suspected Diabetic Macular Oedema (hard exudates within 1 disc diameter of the fovea) is immediately buffered to the emergency specialist queue. Cases with model discordance between ResNet and EfficientNet are also automatically escalated.
  • How does DrishtiTrust ensure offline reliability during rural camps with no 4G?
    The Field Screener portal runs a quantized ONNX model directly inside the browser / local tablet runtime. Local SQLite storage queues patient demographic records and referral slips. As soon as cellular connectivity is detected, encrypted hashes sync with the state health cloud.
  • How is patient data protected under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act?
    All fundus scans are stripped of metadata (EXIF / DICOM headers) before hashing. Only de-identified tokens (e.g. DT-P-0012) are transmitted across public tele-networks. Demographic keys remain isolated inside local camp hardware.
