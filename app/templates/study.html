Ophthalmologist Reader Study Rig

Measuring real-world ophthalmologist validation time and diagnostic accuracy for problem statement SIH26038.

Target: < 30.0 s / Case

Study Protocol & Methodology

Problem statement SIH26038 (MathWorks) calls for clinical AI reports that enable "ophthalmologist validation in under 30 seconds for a human-in-the-loop workflow". No synthetic simulation or test set can prove this — only practicing ophthalmologists interacting with the live review screen can. Participating doctors review 40 grade-stratified retina photographs from public research datasets (Messidor-2 and IDRiD). No hospital patient data is accessed.
